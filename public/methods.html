How the trial works. pre-registered

Every number on the site comes from one engine, placebo/engine.py, run on a committed snapshot of Bitget market data. This page says what it does, with the exact values.

Run a trial

The twin.

Your agent's positions are a grid: long, flat or short, for each market at the close of each 4-hour bar, held over the next bar.

A twin keeps every holding's start, end and size, and redraws its direction with a coin flip. Timing and sizing are identical; only direction is tested.

Placebo draws 2,000 twins with a fixed random seed (seed 7), so the same input always gives the same numbers and a shared link reopens to the same trial.

The p-value.

The agent's annualised Sharpe is compared with the 2,000 twin Sharpes. p = (1 + twins at or above the agent) / 2,001, one-sided. Sharpe uses 2,190 four-hour bars per year.

With several runs, the run with the worst p-value is judged. Consistency is the share of decisions on which every run agrees.

Costs.

Every arm pays Bitget's 0.06% taker fee plus an assumed 0.02% slippage on each position change, and Bitget's recorded funding rate for every funding time inside a held bar.

The comparison arms are a momentum rule (the sign of the last 6 bars' return) and buy and hold.

The verdict rule, fixed before any run.

  • DEPLOY: p at or below 0.05 in every run, Sharpe above the momentum rule, consistency at least 0.80, max drawdown at most 0.20.
  • HALF SIZE: p at or below 0.20 and Sharpe above 0.
  • DO NOT DEPLOY: anything else.

One uploaded run has no consistency figure, so it can reach HALF SIZE at most.

The data.

Bitget USDT-M perpetuals NVDAUSDT, TSLAUSDT and QQQUSDT, 4-hour candles from 1 Jul 2026 to the bar opening 26 Sep 2026 20:00 UTC, 528 bars per market, from api.bitget.com /api/v2/mix/market/candles and /history-candles with productType USDT-FUTURES. Fees from the contracts endpoint, funding from the funding history. The snapshot is committed, so a trial reruns offline.

The reference agent.

Claude Haiku 4.5, two runs, each replayed as four segments that start flat. It sees the last twelve 4-hour returns, 30-bar volatility, whether the US exchange is closed, and its own position, under masked names. It never sees a ticker or a date.

The paper log.

Every 4 hours our machine reads live Bitget prices, asks the same agent for a position, and appends a local-paper row. No exchange orders. The log is published with each deploy.

Uploading your own agent.

A CSV with the header time,symbol,position: time as an ISO date or epoch milliseconds at a 4-hour bar open, symbol NVDA, TSLA or QQQ, position -1, 0 or 1. An optional run column with 2 to 4 values gives one run per value. The file is at most 1 MB.

What this does not tell you.

  1. 88 days is short. A "do not deploy" can mean "not proven yet".
  2. Past bars only. Live fills, funding changes and outages are not in this test.
  3. Results are for these three markets and this window. Different markets need a new trial.
  4. The LLM saw no tickers or dates, and its release predates the window, but hiding names cannot hide every pattern.
