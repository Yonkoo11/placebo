What this does not tell you. outside this trial

  1. 88 days is short. A "do not deploy" can mean "not proven yet".
  2. Past bars only. Live fills, funding changes and outages are not in this test.
  3. Results are for these three markets and this window. Different markets need a new trial.
  4. The LLM saw no tickers or dates, and its release predates the window, but hiding names cannot hide every pattern.

The reference agent is Claude Haiku 4.5 (released October 2025, so it cannot have seen these prices). Every 4 hours it sets a long, flat or short position in three Bitget USDT-M perps: NVDAUSDT, TSLAUSDT and QQQUSDT. It sees the last twelve 4-hour returns, recent volatility, whether the US exchange is closed, and its own position. It never sees a ticker or a price date. It was run twice, and each run replays the window as four segments that each start flat.

Each twin keeps the agent's exact timing and size and redraws the direction of every holding at random. Every arm pays Bitget's 0.06% taker fee, an assumed 0.02% slippage and Bitget's recorded funding.

pre-registeredVerdict rule, fixed before any run: deploy only if the agent beats 95% of twins in every run, beats a simple momentum rule, agrees with itself on at least 80% of decisions, and never draws down more than 20%.
