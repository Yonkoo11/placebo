    How the trial works

    The reference agent is Gemini 3.1 Flash Lite (released May 2026). Every 4 hours it sets a long, flat or short position in three Bitget USDT-M perps: NVDAUSDT, TSLAUSDT and QQQUSDT. It sees the last twelve 4-hour returns, recent volatility, whether the US exchange is closed, and its own position. It never sees a ticker or a date. It was run three times.

    Each twin keeps the agent's exact timing and size and redraws the direction of every holding at random. Every arm pays Bitget's 0.06% taker fee, an assumed 0.02% slippage and Bitget's recorded funding. The verdict rule was fixed before any run: deploy only if the agent beats 95% of twins in every run, beats a simple momentum rule, agrees with itself on at least 80% of decisions, and never draws down more than 20%.

    What this does not tell you

    1. 88 days is short. A “do not deploy” can mean “not proven yet”.
    2. Past bars only. Live fills, funding changes and outages are not in this test.
    3. Results are for these three markets and this window. Different markets need a new trial.
    4. The LLM saw no tickers or dates, and its release predates the window, but hiding names cannot hide every pattern.
    Placebo runs on Bitget's public market data. It places no orders.
